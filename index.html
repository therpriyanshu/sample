Stage 1 of 4

Pre-rinse

A pressure rinse lifts grit and road film before anything touches the paint, so none of it gets dragged into scratches.

Stage 2 of 4

Snow foam

Thick foam covers every panel and sits there, loosening the dirt the rinse left behind. When it slides off, the dirt goes with it.

Stage 3 of 4

Hand wash

Soft mitts and two buckets: one for soapy water, one to rinse the mitt clean between panels. Wheels and tyres get their own brushes.

Stage 4 of 4

Rinse and dry

A final clean-water rinse, then dried by hand with microfibre towels, so you drive away without water spots.
